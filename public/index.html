Privacy Policy & Data Security

Privacy Policy & Voice Data Handling

🎙️

Voice Data & Speech Processing

Alya uses on-device TensorFlow Lite models for wake-word detection ("Alia", "Alya", "Seno"). Microphone access is active strictly when wake detection or live conversation is enabled. We do not store raw voice recordings or log audio clips on external servers.

🔑

Device Permissions

Permissions such as Microphone, Accessibility Service, Notifications, and Overlay display are requested strictly to execute commands you explicitly ask Alya to perform (e.g. taking screenshots, adjusting Wi-Fi, or answering calls).

🛡️

Local Data Storage & Security

Personal preferences, routine schedules, and offline command history are saved locally on your device within an encrypted Room database. No advertising trackers or background data brokers are used.
